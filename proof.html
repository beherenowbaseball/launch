BTB Proof Vault · Public Edition

Proof over promises.

Here’s what actually happened with hitters we coached — and what kind of evidence supports each claim.

We separate third-party documented outcomes from athlete-reported numbers. We do not claim that BTB caused every later opportunity. The facts are strong enough without stretching them.

Third-party documented

Dante Stuart

Independent pro ball → Philadelphia Phillies during the paid coaching window.

Before
One opposite-field home run all prior season.

He described himself as a contact hitter whose hard contact often became ground balls to short. His goal was more power without selling out for it.

Prior-season number: athlete-reported.
11 weeks later
Phillies purchased his contract.

At the time of the signing, the documented line was .363 AVG · 6 HR · 23 RBI · .993 OPS in 31 games. He was assigned to High-A Jersey Shore.

Signing + line: third-party documented. BTB does not claim sole causation.
In the middle of that run, he hit four home runs in six games — all to the opposite field.
School + conference records

Tony Woodie

Before
About .300 with 3 HR.

A 6'4", 240-pound hitter who knew there was more power in him but did not know how to create forward move without losing the swing.

After
School single-season HR + RBI records.

He tied the HR record at nine and broke it with a grand slam, earned all-conference/all-tournament/all-region honors, and opponents intentionally walked him eight times in one week.

Records + honors: third-party documented. Intentional-walk count: athlete-reported on call.
Playing time + scholarship

Parker Sobiesiak

Before
.209 and benched.

He said Instagram and YouTube had him constantly changing things. His father believed baseball might end if the season did not turn around.

After
.350 overall · .421 conference · .614 SLG.

He moved into the middle of the order, earned All-Conference, received a D2 scholarship to Northwest Nazarene, then hit seven home runs the following year after zero in two JUCO seasons and made First Team All-Conference.

Stats, honors and scholarship: third-party documented.
Zero offers → signed

Saul “Mono” Trevino Jr.

Before
Strong enough to bench 350. Still had 0 offers.

He had turned “stay inside the ball” into a rule on every swing, and his strength was not showing up in the ball flight.

After
.407 / .492 / .648 · 1.140 OPS.

He moved from the 6-hole to the 3-hole in four games, hit his first opposite-field and dead-center home runs, received two offers, and signed with Oklahoma Wesleyan.

Stat line + signing: third-party documented. Some EV and sprint details are athlete-reported and are not used here.
The coaching idea: “Stay inside the ball” can be a thought when you need it. It should not become a rule you obey on every single swing.
Recognition

Julian Mercado

Before
15 at-bats at Stetson.

He entered the transfer portal after a season where he felt his opportunity did not match the player he believed he was.

Summer
Futures League All-Star + Home Run Derby selection.

The power showed up without giving away his contact identity. His later JUCO outcome is still open, so we do not claim a final college result.

All-Star + Derby selections: third-party documented.
Opportunity reversal

Spencer Dailey

Before
Formally designated pitcher-only.

He described changing his swing 20–25 times in four years, often copying whoever was hot on social media.

After
Signed by Upper Iowa as offense-first two-way.

He kept hitting on his own after losing the opportunity, and the offer later reversed the role he had been handed.

Signing + offensive designation: documented. He was also working with a separate pitching lab; BTB does not claim recruiting credit for the offer.

How we use proof

We will not turn every good thing that happened later into “BTB did that.”

If an outcome happened outside the coaching window, if another coach or program played a major role, or if a number is only athlete-reported, we say so. That is the standard.

Third-party documented

Stats, signings, school/conference honors, published transactions.

Athlete / parent reported

Useful, specific, and clearly labeled rather than presented as independently verified.

Not used publicly

Private family context, sensitive teen mental-health details, and claims that outrun the evidence.
