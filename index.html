Proof

Different players. Different swings. Different problems.

The goal isn't to make them swing the same. It's to help each hitter bring out the best version of his swing.

Dante Stuart · documented
1 oppo HR all prior season → Phillies contract 11 weeks later

In the middle of the coaching window, Dante hit 4 home runs in 6 games — all opposite field. When Philadelphia purchased his contract, his documented line was .363 / 6 HR / 23 RBI / .993 OPS in 31 games.

Signing + stat line: third-party documented. BTB does not claim sole causation.
Tony Woodie · documented
About .300 / 3 HR → .400 / 10 HR / 57 RBI

He set school single-season HR and RBI records, then earned all-conference, all-tournament and all-region honors. Opponents intentionally walked him eight times in one week.

2026 batting line + records/honors: SUNY Cobleskill. Walk count: athlete-reported.
Parker Sobiesiak · documented
.209 + benched → .350 / .421 conference

Finished with a .614 SLG, earned All-Conference and a D2 scholarship. The next year he hit 7 HR after zero in two JUCO seasons and made First Team All-Conference.

Saul Trevino · documented
