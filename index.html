Proof

Different players. Different swings. Different problems.

The goal isn't to make them swing the same. It's to help each hitter bring out the best version of his swing.

Tony Woodie
3 HR → school single-season HR & RBI records

Started as a converted pitcher with 3 home runs the prior season. Later set both school records and earned all-conference, all-tournament and all-region honors.

Parker Sobiesiak
.209 + benched → .350 / .421 conference

Finished with a .614 slug, earned All-Conference and a D2 scholarship. The next year he hit 7 HR after zero in two JUCO seasons.

Saul Trevino
0 offers → .400+ season → signed

Moved from the 6-hole to the 3-hole, hit his first opposite-field and dead-center home runs, received two offers and signed with Oklahoma Wesleyan.

Spencer Dailey
Pitcher-only → offense-first two-way signing

After being designated PO, he kept training as a hitter and later signed with Upper Iowa as an offensive player first, pitching secondary.

Ryan Foster
Confidence 1–2 → most improved player

Redshirted and nearly cut. His first fall at-bat was a home run, he reached 97.6 mph EV, climbed the outfield depth chart and was named most improved.

Brady Lester
103 max → 105 at ~80% effort

A strong NAIA player stuck in a two-year tinkering loop. Within two weeks he set a new EV PR at reduced effort and later committed to Ohio State.

BTB coaching ended about four months before the Ohio State commitment. We do not claim BTB handled that recruitment.
