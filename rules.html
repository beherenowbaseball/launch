BTB 30-Day Accelerator Official Rules

Effective September 24, 2026 · Sponsor: Be The Best Baseball LLC, Texas
No purchase necessary. Applying or purchasing anything from BTB does not improve your chance of being selected. This is a skill/fit-based promotional selection, not a random drawing.

1. Eligibility

Open to baseball players age 13 or older who are legally permitted to participate. Applicants ages 13–17 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian and provide parent or guardian contact information. Employees, contractors directly administering the promotion, and their immediate household members are not eligible to win.

2. How to enter

Submit one complete BTB 30-Day Accelerator application through the official Typeform linked from this site during the announced application window. Limit one application per athlete unless BTB explicitly invites an update.

3. Selection

BTB plans to select three athletes based on the application, coaching fit, the athlete’s stated goals and challenges, willingness to follow the program and send video, and BTB’s belief that it can meaningfully help the athlete during the 30-day period. Selection is not random. BTB may contact finalists or a parent/guardian before confirming a spot.

4. Prize

Each selected athlete receives 30 days inside the BTB Athlete Accelerator as described on the launch page. The prize is coaching access only, has no cash alternative, is non-transferable, and may not be sold. Any equipment, travel, facility fees, internet access, or third-party costs are the athlete’s responsibility unless BTB states otherwise in writing.

5. Participation expectations

Selected athletes are expected to train at least twice per week, follow reasonable coaching recommendations, communicate honestly, and regularly submit useful video so BTB can coach them. BTB may end complimentary participation for safety issues, abusive behavior, non-participation, misrepresentation, or conduct that materially disrupts the program.

6. Results are not guaranteed

Baseball development varies by athlete. The free promotional coaching spot does not guarantee a particular exit velocity, batting average, scholarship, roster spot, recruiting result, professional opportunity, or other outcome.

7. Publicity and testimonials

Winning does not automatically grant BTB the right to use an athlete’s name, image, video, testimonial, or results in advertising. BTB will obtain any additional permission or release it reasonably requires before using identifiable athlete media for promotional purposes. For minors, parent or guardian approval may be required.

8. Changes or cancellation

BTB may modify, suspend, or cancel the promotion if technical issues, fraud, safety concerns, platform failures, legal requirements, or circumstances outside BTB’s reasonable control materially affect the promotion. If that happens, BTB may select recipients from eligible applications already received where practical.

9. General conditions

By applying, entrants agree to these Official Rules and to BTB’s reasonable decisions in administering the promotion. The promotion is void where prohibited. Nothing in these rules waives rights that cannot legally be waived.

10. Contact

Questions about the promotion can be sent to beherenowbaseball@gmail.com.
