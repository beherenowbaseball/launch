FAQ

Questions

Is this a course?

No. You get the BTB curriculum, but the Accelerator includes personalized coaching, reviews, a private Discord channel and group calls.

How often do I need to train?

At least two times per week. The more consistently you train and send useful video, the more we have to coach.

Do I have to change my swing?

No. The point is not to rebuild you. We test movements and feels, keep what improves your swing, and discard what doesn't.

What happens after 30 days?

Your Accelerator ends. If you want to continue and we believe BTB is a good fit, we'll show you the 90-day Core or higher-touch coaching options.

What if the group is full?

Join next month's waitlist. We keep each group intentionally small so the coaching remains personal.
