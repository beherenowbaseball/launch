Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026 · Be The Best Baseball LLC
Short version: We collect the information you give us so we can administer the BTB application, communicate with you, deliver coaching, process payments, and improve BTB. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with BTB, we may collect your name, email, mobile number, Instagram handle, age category, player level, hitting goals and challenges, parent or guardian contact information for minors, application responses, coaching videos or messages you choose to send, purchase information, and technical information such as device, browser, referral source, and page activity.

How we use it

  • Review and administer applications, waitlists, contests, and coaching programs.
  • Contact applicants, athletes, and parents or guardians about BTB.
  • Provide coaching, onboarding, customer support, and program access.
  • Send marketing emails or messages where permitted and honor opt-out requests.
  • Process transactions and maintain business records.
  • Measure site and campaign performance and improve our programs.
  • Protect BTB, our users, and our systems from misuse or fraud.

Service providers

We may use service providers for forms, CRM, email, messaging, analytics, hosting, payments, community, and coaching delivery. These providers process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy obligations. Current tools may include Typeform and Stripe, and BTB may add or replace service providers over time.

Minors

The Accelerator application is intended for athletes age 13 or older. Applicants ages 13–17 must provide parent or guardian information so we can keep an adult in the loop. We do not knowingly invite children under 13 to submit the application. If you believe a child under 13 submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Marketing choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in those messages. You may also ask us to update or remove your information, subject to records we must retain for legitimate business, legal, security, or accounting purposes.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above and use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect it. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how your personal information is used. We will respond to applicable requests as required by law.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to beherenowbaseball@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as BTB changes. The effective date above shows the latest version posted on this site.
